# Discord asset library

[Ocean assets](ocean/README.md) includes water photography and transparent fish and bubble sprites sourced for Jade Teahouse.

79 Pinterest downloads (7 supplied references and 72 additional finds), plus 2 transparent PNG variants and 35 transparent character images. The working library lives under `design/`.

Banners and dividers fit wide Discord image slots. Stickers and illustrations can be cropped for thumbnails. Sprite sheets and references are useful when planning a design.

Pinterest entries link to the exact image and, where available, its Pin or collection. Original creators and reuse permissions have not been verified for every asset. Transparent variants are ImageGen edits of the linked originals. Character entries include the creator and the terms listed by the source. Animal Crossing and Kirby artwork is cataloged as copyrighted reference art.
